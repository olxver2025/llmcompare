\# LLMcompare Providers



Reference guide for identifying providers, model slug patterns, sibling model families, the latest models represented in the LLMcompare catalog, and the official source to monitor for new releases.



> \*\*Last checked:\*\* 2026-08-13

> \*\*Primary repo source:\*\* \[`src/data/models.ts`](https://github.com/olxver2025/llmcompare/blob/master/src/data/models.ts)

>

> \*\*Important:\*\* “Latest 3” means the three newest release-dated models represented in the LLMcompare dataset. Some entries may be previews, restricted models, self-hosted/open-weight releases, or models available only through specific products/providers.

\# Maintenance Rule



When adding a new model to LLMcompare:



1\. Check the provider's official news/research page above.

2\. Check the provider's official model/API documentation.

3\. Preserve the provider's \*\*actual canonical model slug\*\* where one exists.

4\. Record the model's exact release date.

5\. Keep sibling models under the same family naming convention.

6\. Prefer first-party sources for announcement URLs, specifications and pricing.

7\. Do not treat a third-party hosting alias as the provider's canonical slug.

8\. For open-weight models, distinguish between the upstream model name and any provider-specific hosted alias.

9\. Treat preview, private-beta, research-only, app-only and self-hosted models as distinct availability states rather than silently calling them generally available.
